Our Mission

At Rafeeq, we believe in empowering individuals through personalized career guidance. Our mission is to connect job seekers, students, and career switchers with experienced professionals who can offer tailored advice, CV feedback, and realistic mock interviews that reflect the local job market.

About Rafeeq

Rafeeq was born from the need for a structured, accessible mentorship and interview preparation platform tailored to the Egyptian and Arab job market. We identified a gap: job seekers often rely on informal sources or generic global platforms that don’t understand the cultural context or hiring landscape here. So, we created Rafeeq — a professional, easy-to-use platform where users can book sessions with verified mentors and interview coaches, receive real-time feedback, and prepare confidently for their next career move.

Why Choose Rafeeq?

Rafeeq offers a range of features designed to help mentees improve their job readiness and give mentors a reliable way to share knowledge and earn income. From CV reviews to mock interviews, our tools are built to support your journey toward success.

Find Verified Mentors

Search for mentors by industry, experience, language, and availability. All mentors go through a verification process to ensure quality and professionalism.

Book Mock Interviews

Prepare for your dream job with realistic mock interviews led by experienced professionals. Get instant feedback and build confidence before the real thing.

CV & Resume Feedback

Upload your CV and get expert tips from mentors to make it stand out to recruiters and pass automated screening systems (ATS).
